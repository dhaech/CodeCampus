Professor FAQs

How do I create a new course?

Hit the "Create Course" button, then fill in the course details to publish it.

How do I add activities with correct answers?

Use the "Add Activities" tab beside a course to set the problem statement and test case.

How can I track students in a course?

Click on the "Student" tab in a private course to see who joined and monitor their progress.

Can I monitor student performance?

Yes, detailed analytics are available under the course dashboard to track student performance.
